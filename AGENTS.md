- Design pages as experiences, as on /decarbonize-india: give each section one interaction or motion that carries its meaning (scroll-driven story, before/after reveal, draw-in doodles, count-ups) instead of generic card grids, and choose a different pattern per page so pages do not look alike. Keep facts sourced, respect prefers-reduced-motion with a still final state, and allow no horizontal scroll at 390px.
- Kill word REVERT (optionally with a page name, e.g. "REVERT pricing"): undo that page's most recent redesign exactly, with `git revert -m 1 <merge commit>` of its PR in a new PR, and merge it. Do not patch, restyle or "fix forward" on top, and do not combine it with other changes. Each page redesign therefore ships as its own PR whose body records the main commit it started from; see docs/runbooks/design-revert.md.
- Prefer cleaner pages: keep fact sources in code comments or data files, not as visible reference links or citation lines on the page, unless the user asks for them.
- Never expose ITR, income tax returns or financial statements: do not show them on the Dashboard or any page, and do not parse them without explicit approval. They are reserved for later server-side climate risk and underwriting, under strict data-protection rules across countries.
- Electricity bills: read only billing details (period, tariff category, sanctioned load, units); never extract consumer name, address, phone, consumer, account or meter numbers. Keep extra bill fields inside documents.cached_result, so no schema change is needed.
