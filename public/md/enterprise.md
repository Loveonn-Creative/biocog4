# Supplier emissions, without the chasing

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/enterprise

Agents collect what your suppliers already have and turn it into Scope 3 records you can audit. Live today: documents to verified records. Being built: the supplier network. Every capability below is marked Live, Pilot or Roadmap.

## How the agents work

Invite, collect, clean, verify, report. Most suppliers start from documents and estimates; sensors upgrade the data later.

- Invite (Roadmap): Supplier invitations and portal
- Collect (Pilot): Documents today; other channels later
- Clean (Live): Line items extracted and classified to Scope 1, 2 or 3
- Verify (Live): Hash, factor, confidence score
- Report (Live): For one entity today; supplier roll-up later

### Inputs

- Invoices, bills and PDFs read by AI (Live): Images and PDFs, one record per document
- Bulk document upload (Live): Up to 10 files per batch today
- CSV or spreadsheet import (Roadmap)
- ERP feeds (Tally, SAP, Oracle, Zoho) (Roadmap)
- SFTP drop (Roadmap)
- Documents by email or WhatsApp (Roadmap)
- Meters and sensors, where a supplier has them (Roadmap)
- API and webhooks (Roadmap)

## Scale without breaking

Primary data where the spend is. Spend-based estimates for the long tail. People only see what the checks flag.

- Supplier tiering from your invoices (Live): Primary, factor-derived or spend-only
- Data-quality view per supplier (Pilot): Tier and named gaps; numeric score per document
- Exception queue for flagged records (Pilot): Needs-review status today; queue and assignment later
- Onboarding in waves (Roadmap)
- Automatic supplier reminders (Roadmap)
- Duplicate documents caught by hash (Live)
- Idempotent verification (no double counting) (Live)
- Queued, rate-limited ingestion for large uploads (Roadmap)

## The supplier keeps its data

- Row-level isolation of every record (Live): Per account today
- Supplier owns its record and consents per buyer (Roadmap)
- Buyers receive figures and proof hashes, not invoices (Roadmap)
- Team roles (owner, admin, analyst, viewer) (Pilot): Team management today
- Audit log (Pilot)
- Single sign-on (SAML, OIDC) (Roadmap)

No third-party security certification is claimed. Security detail is on [Trust and technical validation](https://senseible.earth/trust).

## Every number has a trail

- Each figure links to its document hash, factor and method version (Live)
- Confidence score on every record (Live)
- GHG Protocol, BRSR and BRSR Core, CBAM, ISO 14064, GRI 305, CDP (C6), ESRS (E1-5, E1-6) reports (Live): For one entity, labelled self-reported
- Audit-trail export (XLSX) (Live)
- Named reviewer sign-off on records (Roadmap)
- Roll-up across hundreds of suppliers (Roadmap)
