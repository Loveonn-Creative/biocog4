# Green loans for MSMEs in India, compared

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/green-loans-msme-india

SIDBI, IREDA, banks and NBFCs each lend for different green projects, with different limits and paperwork, and most do not publish a rate. Pick what the money is for and see who lends for it, how much, and what they ask.

Senseible makes one of the tools on this list. Senseible does not lend, arrange or approve loans. Checked 29 October 2026.

## The list

### 1. SIDBI End to End Energy Efficiency (4E)

- Who it is for: Established MSMEs with three years of cash profit.
- Best for: A profitable unit replacing inefficient equipment without putting in its own money.
- Rate or cost: Rate not published; ask SIDBI.
- Strengths: Can fund the whole project cost. Covers civil works and commissioning, not just equipment.
- Limits: Needs three profitable years. Rate not published.

### 2. IREDA energy efficiency loans

- Who it is for: Companies and LLPs with larger energy-efficiency projects; ESCOs.
- Best for: Mid-sized companies and ESCOs financing energy-efficiency retrofits.
- Rate or cost: Grade-based rates; see Financing Norms.
- Strengths: Accepts ESCO and shared-savings projects. BEE grading and risk guarantee support.
- Limits: Rs 50 lakh minimum. Proprietors and partnerships need full bank guarantee or FD.

### 3. SIDBI Green Finance Scheme (GFS)

- Who it is for: New and existing Udyam-registered MSMEs, plus solar and green service providers or aggregators serving MSMEs.
- Best for: A new or growing MSME funding a broad green project, or an aggregator funding many small ones.
- Rate or cost: Rate not published; ask SIDBI.
- Strengths: Open to new units, not only those with three years' history. Large ceiling and open to aggregators.
- Limits: At least 25% must come from you. Rate and security only in the sanction letter.

### 4. IREDA rooftop solar loan scheme

- Who it is for: Aggregators and RESCOs; large roof owners.
- Best for: Solar aggregators financing many MSME rooftops.
- Rate or cost: Grade-based rates.
- Strengths: Aggregator route pools many small roofs. Clear published norms.
- Limits: 5,000 kWp minimum excludes a single MSME roof. No subsidy from IREDA.

### 5. SIDBI machinery loans (EXPRESS, SPEED, UDAY)

- Who it is for: MSMEs buying new machines; UDAY suits units under three years old.
- Best for: Replacing old machines with efficient new ones when a green scheme does not fit.
- Rate or cost: Rate not published.
- Strengths: UDAY serves new units that green loans with vintage rules exclude. Fast, digitally assessed EXPRESS route.
- Limits: Not a green product; no energy saving needed or rewarded. Rates not published.

### 6. SIDBI GCF-FMAP (Green Climate Fund loans)

- Who it is for: MSMEs in the listed sectors with low or medium environmental risk.
- Best for: Units in textile, foundry, plastic, brick, paper or steel re-rolling buying listed efficient equipment.
- Rate or cost: Not published.
- Strengths: Sector-specific equipment lists make eligibility concrete. Uses Green Climate Fund money.
- Limits: Limited to listed sectors. Amounts and rates not published.

### 7. RBI rules on MSME collateral

- Who it is for: Any micro or small enterprise borrowing from a bank.
- Best for: Small units pushing back when a bank asks for property on a small green loan.
- Strengths: Collateral-free floor for small loans. 14-working-day decision limit for loans up to Rs 25 lakh.
- Limits: Not a scheme; you still need a lender. Above Rs 25 lakh, each bank's own timelines apply.

### 8. MSE-GIFT and MSE-SPICE (RAMP schemes listed by SIDBI)

- Who it is for: Micro and small enterprises adopting green technology or circular practices.
- Best for: MSEs taking a green loan who want to check for an interest incentive on top.
- Rate or cost: Not published.
- Strengths: Government support that lowers the cost of a green loan. Covers circular economy projects other schemes miss.
- Limits: Whether the window is still open is unclear; ask the lender. Support rate and cap not on the page.

### 9. NBFC green lenders (RBI green deposit framework)

- Who it is for: Borrowers of deposit-taking NBFCs that raise green deposits.
- Best for: Borrowers comparing NBFC green products against bank and SIDBI routes.
- Strengths: NBFCs may lend green whether or not they raise green deposits. Published frameworks show what each lender counts as green.
- Limits: Terms vary by lender; check each lender’s own framework. Expect end-use checks and reporting.

### 10. Senseible green loan check (readiness step)

- Who it is for: Indian MSMEs preparing a green loan application.
- Best for: MSMEs getting their papers and emissions evidence ready before approaching a lender.
- Rate or cost: The check is free. Application prep on Essential and higher, from ₹499 a month billed yearly, plus GST.
- Strengths: Produces the bill-based evidence several schemes ask for (12 months of bills). Two-minute checklist of what a credit team will ask.
- Limits: Not a lender; the bank or SIDBI decides. A checklist, not an eligibility decision.

## Which tools fit which situation

- Energy-efficiency upgrade: SIDBI End to End Energy Efficiency (4E), IREDA energy efficiency loans, SIDBI Green Finance Scheme (GFS)
- Rooftop solar: IREDA rooftop solar loan scheme, SIDBI Green Finance Scheme (GFS), SIDBI End to End Energy Efficiency (4E)
- New or replacement machinery: SIDBI End to End Energy Efficiency (4E), SIDBI machinery loans (EXPRESS, SPEED, UDAY), SIDBI GCF-FMAP (Green Climate Fund loans)
- Unit under three years old: SIDBI Green Finance Scheme (GFS), SIDBI machinery loans (EXPRESS, SPEED, UDAY), SIDBI End to End Energy Efficiency (4E)
- Small loan, no property to pledge: RBI rules on MSME collateral, SIDBI End to End Energy Efficiency (4E), IREDA energy efficiency loans
- Getting papers ready first: Senseible green loan check (readiness step), IREDA energy efficiency loans, SIDBI GCF-FMAP (Green Climate Fund loans)

## How to choose

1. Which green projects it pays for. Ask: "Is our exact equipment or project on the eligible list?"
2. Published loan or cover limits. Ask: "What is the minimum and maximum, and does our project fit?"
3. Share of project cost financed (the rest is your margin). Ask: "How much do we put in ourselves?"
4. Who qualifies: years running, profits, Udyam, credit history. Ask: "How many years of profit do you need to see?"
5. Repayment period. Ask: "How long to repay, and is there a moratorium?"
6. Interest rate or fee published. Ask: "What rate, and is it fixed or linked to a benchmark?"
7. How to apply: online portal, branch or referral partner. Ask: "Can we apply online, and who helps us fill it in?"
8. Energy, emission or environmental evidence the lender asks for. Ask: "Which bills, audits or reports do you want with the application?"
