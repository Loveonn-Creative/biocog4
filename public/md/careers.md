# Careers at Senseible: Build Climate Infrastructure

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/careers

Open roles at Senseible across engineering, applied AI, climate research, design and growth, plus intern and fresher tracks. See how to apply.

## Open roles

- Founding Engineer (Engineering, Senior, Gurugram): Own core MRV pipeline, deterministic carbon math, and the trust layer end-to-end.
- Applied AI Engineer (AI & Research, Mid, Hybrid): Ship document extraction, voice interfaces, and Scope 3 inference against real MSME data.
- Climate Research Associate (Climate & Carbon, Mid, Remote (India)): Own emission factor governance, methodology versioning, CBAM/BRSR alignment, and audit defensibility.
- Product Designer (Design, Mid, Hybrid): Design calm, regulator-safe surfaces used by MSME owners, auditors, and lenders in one flow.
- Growth & Operations (Growth & Operations, Mid, Gurugram): Move partners, lenders, and MSMEs from first touch to signed pilot without a sales machine.
- Founder's Office (Founder's Office, Mid, Gurugram): Sit next to the founders. Run the hardest un-owned problem each week: research, strategy, execution.
- Freshers: Engineering, Research, Design, Ops (Engineering, Fresher, Hybrid): Open track for recent graduates. Ship real work in week one. High ownership, direct mentorship.
- Interns: Applied AI, Climate, Product (AI & Research, Intern, Remote (India)): 3–6 month projects with real production scope. Strong performers convert to full-time.

Freshers and interns apply through the intake form on the Careers page; everyone else can email build@senseible.earth.
