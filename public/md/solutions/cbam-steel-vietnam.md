# CBAM Compliance for Steel Manufacturing in Vietnam

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/cbam-steel-vietnam

Vietnamese steel sold to the EU now carries a CBAM cost for the importer on every tonne above the free-allocation adjustment. Scrap-based electric arc furnace mills can sit below the benchmark in early years; blast-furnace mills cannot.

## Questions

### Who pays CBAM, the exporter or the EU buyer?

The EU importer, as an authorised CBAM declarant, buys and surrenders certificates; the first annual declaration for 2026 imports is due by 30 September 2027. The exporter supplies verified emissions data per tonne so the importer does not have to use default values with a mark-up.

### Does a scrap-based EAF mill owe anything in 2026?

If its verified intensity is below the adjusted EU benchmark, the 2026 estimate is zero. As free allocation falls to 0% in 2034, a cost appears, but it stays far below blast-furnace steel.
