Bank Negara Malaysia Climate Change and Principle-based Taxonomy (CCPT) and green sukuk market offer financing for manufacturers with verified carbon baselines.

## Questions

### What green finance is available for Malaysian manufacturers?

Bank Negara CCPT classifies green activities. Green sukuk, SRI funds, and commercial bank green loans are available. Verified carbon data is the key eligibility criterion.
