# Fleet Carbon Audit for Logistics & Shipping in Philippines

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/carbon-audit-logistics-philippines

Philippine logistics companies need fleet emission data for ISO 14083 compliance and international shipping carbon reporting requirements.

## Steps

1. **Upload Utility Bills:** Upload electricity, fuel, and water invoices from your logistics operations in Philippines.
2. **Auto-Classify Emissions:** Senseible AI classifies each line item into Scope 1, 2, or 3 with confidence scores.
3. **Review Trust Score:** Check data quality indicators and resolve any flagged inconsistencies before submission.
4. **Download Audit Report:** Generate a compliance-ready carbon audit report with full traceability to source documents.
