# Scope 3 Emissions for Palm Oil & Food in Malaysia

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/scope3-food-processing-malaysia

Malaysia produces 25% of global palm oil. EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) and CSRD require emission data across the palm oil value chain from plantation to refinery.

1. **Map Value Chain:** Identify all upstream and downstream emission sources in your food processing supply chain in Malaysia.

## Questions

### How are palm oil Scope 3 emissions measured in Malaysia?

RSPO-aligned methodology covers plantation land use, mill processing, refinery energy, and transportation. Land use change emissions are the largest component.
