# Scope 3 Reporting for Chemical SMEs in India

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/scope3-chemicals-india

Chemical buyers and lenders ask for value-chain emissions, especially purchased feedstocks and freight. Start from purchase and logistics invoices, and calculate your own Scope 1 and 2 from fuel and power records.

## Steps

1. **Map Value Chain:** Identify all upstream and downstream emission sources in your chemicals supply chain in India.
2. **Collect Supplier Data:** Upload invoices from raw material suppliers, logistics providers, and service partners.
3. **Apply Emission Factors:** Use India-specific IEA grid factors and sector benchmarks for accurate Scope 3 calculations.
4. **Generate GHG Report:** Create GHG Protocol-compliant Scope 3 reports with category-wise breakdowns.

## Questions

### Are my chemicals covered by CBAM?

Only ammonia, nitric acid, nitrogen and mixed fertilisers, potassium nitrates and hydrogen are CBAM goods. Other chemicals are not, but buyers may still ask for product footprints.
