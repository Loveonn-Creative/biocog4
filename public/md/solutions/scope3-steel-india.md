# Scope 3 Calculator for Steel Manufacturing in India

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/scope3-steel-india

Steel buyers and lenders increasingly ask for value-chain emissions: purchased iron ore, coal, ferro-alloys and freight. Measuring them starts with purchase invoices and supplier data, with Scope 1 and 2 calculated from fuel and power bills.

## Steps

1. **Map Value Chain:** Identify all upstream and downstream emission sources in your steel supply chain in India.
2. **Collect Supplier Data:** Upload invoices from raw material suppliers, logistics providers, and service partners.
3. **Apply Emission Factors:** Use India-specific IEA grid factors and sector benchmarks for accurate Scope 3 calculations.
4. **Generate GHG Report:** Create GHG Protocol-compliant Scope 3 reports with category-wise breakdowns.

## Questions

### Is Scope 3 needed for CBAM?

No. CBAM uses installation-level direct emissions (and precursors) per product. Scope 3 is what corporate buyers and lenders ask for.
