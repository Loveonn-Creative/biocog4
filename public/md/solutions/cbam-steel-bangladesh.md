# CBAM Compliance for Steel & Metal in Bangladesh

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/cbam-steel-bangladesh

Bangladeshi steel shipped to the EU is a CBAM good from 1 January 2026. Re-rolling mills that buy billets must account for the billets' embedded emissions as well as their own, so supplier data matters as much as plant data.

## Questions

### Who pays CBAM, the exporter or the EU buyer?

The EU importer, as an authorised CBAM declarant, buys and surrenders certificates; the first annual declaration for 2026 imports is due by 30 September 2027. The exporter supplies verified emissions data per tonne so the importer does not have to use default values with a mark-up.

### Does the 50-tonne exemption help?

Only at the importer level: an EU importer bringing in 50 tonnes or less of covered goods in a year is exempt. Once it exceeds 50 tonnes, all of that year's imports are covered.
