# Carbon Audit for Manufacturing SMEs in India

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/carbon-audit-manufacturing-india

A carbon audit sets your baseline: Scope 1 from fuel, Scope 2 from electricity, and the main Scope 3 sources from purchases. It is the starting point for buyer questionnaires, BRSR customers and green loan applications.

## Steps

1. **Upload Utility Bills:** Upload electricity, fuel, and water invoices from your manufacturing operations in India.
2. **Auto-Classify Emissions:** Senseible AI classifies each line item into Scope 1, 2, or 3 with confidence scores.
3. **Review Trust Score:** Check data quality indicators and resolve any flagged inconsistencies before submission.
4. **Download Audit Report:** Generate a compliance-ready carbon audit report with full traceability to source documents.

## Questions

### What documents do I need for a carbon audit?

Electricity bills, fuel purchase invoices, freight receipts and raw material purchase orders for the reporting period, usually 12 months.
