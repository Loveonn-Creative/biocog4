## Questions

### Are Indian textile exports covered under CBAM?

Not directly in the current CBAM scope, but EU CSRD requires large importers to report Scope 3 emissions from their supply chain, which includes Indian textile suppliers.

### Why should textile MSMEs prepare for carbon reporting now?

EU buyers are already requesting emission data from suppliers. Early movers get preferred vendor status and access to green finance.
