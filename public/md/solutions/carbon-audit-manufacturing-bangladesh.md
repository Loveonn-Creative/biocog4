# Carbon Audit for Manufacturing SMEs in Bangladesh

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/carbon-audit-manufacturing-bangladesh

Bangladeshi manufacturers face growing pressure from international buyers for emission disclosures. The DoE-ECA framework requires environmental compliance but lacks carbon-specific guidance for SMEs.

## Steps

1. **Upload Utility Bills:** Upload electricity, fuel, and water invoices from your manufacturing operations in Bangladesh.
2. **Auto-Classify Emissions:** Senseible AI classifies each line item into Scope 1, 2, or 3 with confidence scores.
3. **Review Trust Score:** Check data quality indicators and resolve any flagged inconsistencies before submission.
4. **Download Audit Report:** Generate a compliance-ready carbon audit report with full traceability to source documents.
