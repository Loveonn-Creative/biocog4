# Scope 3 Reporting for Textile & Apparel in Pakistan

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/scope3-textile-pakistan

Pakistan is the 5th largest cotton producer. EU buyers demand Scope 3 data from Pakistani textile suppliers. Without compliance, $3.2B in EU textile exports are at risk.
