# CBAM Compliance for Chemical Industry in India

## Questions

### Who pays CBAM, the exporter or the EU buyer?

The EU importer, as an authorised CBAM declarant, buys and surrenders certificates; the first annual declaration for 2026 imports is due by 30 September 2027. The exporter supplies verified emissions data per tonne so the importer does not have to use default values with a mark-up.

### Which chemical products are covered?

Ammonia (CN 2814), nitric acid (2808 00 00), potassium nitrates (2834 21 00), nitrogen fertilisers (3102), mixed fertilisers (3105, except 3105 60 00) and hydrogen (2804 10 00). Dyes, specialty and most organic chemicals are not covered.

### What mark-up applies to fertiliser default values?

1% from 2026, under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2621, compared with 10% to 30% for steel, aluminium, cement and hydrogen.
