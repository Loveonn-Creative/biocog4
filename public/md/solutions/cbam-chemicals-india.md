# CBAM Compliance for Chemical Industry in India

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/cbam-chemicals-india

Indian chemical exporters shipping to the EU face CBAM declarations starting 2026. Fertilizers, hydrogen, and basic chemicals are directly covered under EU CBAM regulation.

## Questions

### Which Indian chemical exports are CBAM-covered?

Ammonia (CN 2814), urea (CN 310210), ammonium nitrate (CN 310230), nitric acid (CN 2808), and hydrogen (CN 280410) are directly covered.
