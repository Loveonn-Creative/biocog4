# CBAM Readiness for Chemical Industry in Vietnam

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/cbam-chemicals-vietnam

Vietnamese ammonia, nitrogen fertiliser and hydrogen exports to the EU are CBAM goods from 2026. Most other chemicals are not covered, so the first step is checking each product against the CN codes.

## Questions

### Who pays CBAM, the exporter or the EU buyer?

The EU importer, as an authorised CBAM declarant, buys and surrenders certificates; the first annual declaration for 2026 imports is due by 30 September 2027. The exporter supplies verified emissions data per tonne so the importer does not have to use default values with a mark-up.

### Is hydrogen subject to the 50-tonne exemption?

No. Importers of hydrogen and electricity are always covered, whatever the volume.
