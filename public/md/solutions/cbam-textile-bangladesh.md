# CBAM Readiness for Textile Manufacturers in Bangladesh

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/solutions/cbam-textile-bangladesh

While textiles are not yet in CBAM scope, Bangladesh textile exporters face indirect pressure from EU CSRD supply chain reporting requirements affecting their European buyers.

## Questions

### How does CBAM affect Bangladeshi textiles?

Indirectly through CSRD. EU brands must report Scope 3 emissions which includes Bangladeshi supplier data. Factories without carbon data risk losing orders.
