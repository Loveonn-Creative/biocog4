# Carbon compliance software, compared

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/best-carbon-compliance-software

CSRD, ISSB, BRSR, India’s carbon market and California’s SB 253 ask for overlapping numbers in different formats. The right tool covers the rules you actually face, with an audit trail that holds up. Pick the rules you report under.

Senseible makes one of the tools on this list. Checked 29 October 2026.

## The list

### 1. TSC NetZero

- Who it is for: Indian listed enterprises and exporters; also banks (PCAF) and EU importers (CBAM).
- Best for: Indian listed groups that report to BRSR Core and also face CSRD or ISSB requests.
- Pricing: On request for reporting modules (CBAM importer prices are published separately).
- Strengths: Covers Indian (BRSR, CCTS) and global (CSRD, ISSB) frameworks with question mapping across them. XBRL output for BRSR Core and plant-system integrations (SCADA, DCS, BMS, IoT).
- Limits: No SB 253 coverage found. Some CBAM pages still describe transitional quarterly reports.

### 2. Carbon-OS

- Who it is for: Listed companies, lenders, CBAM exporters and OEM supply chains; enterprise.
- Best for: Indian enterprises with several regimes at once that need figures an assurer can trace.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Rule statements on its pages are current (CSRD Omnibus, BRSR assessment-or-assurance, CBAM definitive period). One evidence ledger feeds BRSR Core, CCTS, CBAM, CSRD and ISSB.
- Limits: No SB 253 coverage found. No published prices.

### 3. Intellocarbon

- Who it is for: Indian industrial companies and MSMEs.
- Best for: Indian MSMEs that want many frameworks from one data entry at MSME cost.
- Pricing: Not published.
- Strengths: One subscription covers BRSR Core, ISSB, GRI, CSRD/ESRS and CDP plus CBAM and CCTS in other plans. Built for Indian MSMEs.
- Limits: States BRSR Core needs reasonable assurance; SEBI's March 2025 circular allows assessment or assurance. CBAM pages still describe quarterly reporting.

### 4. Sweep

- Who it is for: Large companies in CSRD, ISSB and SB 253 scope.
- Best for: Multinationals reporting to CSRD, ISSB and California at once.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: EU and US frameworks (CSRD, ISSB, SB 253) from one data set with digital tagging. Named a leader by IDC MarketScape 2026 and Verdantix 2026, per Sweep.
- Limits: No India frameworks (BRSR, CCTS) found. No published prices.

### 5. Persefoni

- Who it is for: Businesses of all sizes; financial institutions (PCAF).
- Best for: US-linked companies and financial firms preparing for California and ISSB.
- Pricing: Free plan; paid plan on request.
- Strengths: Free starting plan with Scope 1 to 3. US coverage (SB 253, SB 261) and financed emissions.
- Limits: Framework reports are GHG-metric reports; no India coverage found. Several features marked 'coming soon' on the pricing page.

### 6. osapiens HUB Disclosures and Reporting

- Who it is for: Mid-market and enterprise organisations worldwide.
- Best for: European groups that must file tagged CSRD statements.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Certified XBRL tagging and a reporting cockpit that updates the report from the data. Covers CSRD, VSME, GRI, ISSB, HKFRS SDS and UK SRS.
- Limits: No India or California frameworks found. No prices.

### 7. Sprih

- Who it is for: Large enterprises and their value chains across industries.
- Best for: Indian and US enterprises wanting AI-drafted disclosures with peer benchmarks.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Free drafted report lets a buyer test output before paying. BRSR filing checker compares XBRL against the PDF.
- Limits: ISSB not named on its reporting page. No CCTS or published prices.

### 8. Karbon

- Who it is for: Hard-to-abate and listed enterprises; SME programmes; India, UAE, Oman, Bangladesh.
- Best for: Indian public-sector and infrastructure enterprises that want software and delivery together.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: On-premise deployment option. Software plus consulting, training and assurance support.
- Limits: Framework coverage described at headline level only. No prices.

### 9. IBM Envizi ESG Suite

- Who it is for: Enterprises with large ESG data volumes.
- Best for: Global enterprises consolidating ESG data at scale, especially real estate and multi-site groups.
- Pricing: On request; an indicative calculator by account bundle: Essentials (up to 1,000 accounts), Standard (1,001 to 5,000), Premium (5,001 to 15,000+). Minimum term 12 months.
- Strengths: Over 1,000 framework questions with answer reuse across years and frameworks. Scales to very large data volumes.
- Limits: ISSB, BRSR, CCTS and SB 253 not named on its site. Enterprise pricing via sales.

### 10. Updapt

- Who it is for: Enterprises in India and the UAE.
- Best for: Indian and Gulf enterprises wanting BRSR and CSRD in one tool.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Free trial. India and UAE framework focus with double materiality and supplier assessment modules.
- Limits: Little product detail is published on its site. No CCTS or SB 253 found.

### 11. Senseible

- Who it is for: MSMEs and suppliers in India that need emission records from their own bills; not a multi-framework disclosure suite.
- Best for: Suppliers and MSMEs answering a listed customer's emissions request, not compliance teams filing several frameworks.
- Pricing: Free Snapshot plan. Paid plans from ₹499 a month billed yearly, plus GST. Scale on request.
- Strengths: Every figure traced to a bill with factor and source. Low published prices with a free plan.
- Limits: Not a multi-framework disclosure suite; reports are self-declared drafts from your own records. BRSR coverage limited to energy and emissions fields.

## Which tools fit which situation

- Indian listed company (BRSR, CCTS): TSC NetZero, Carbon-OS, Karbon
- EU group under CSRD: TSC NetZero, Sweep, osapiens HUB Disclosures and Reporting
- US-linked: California SB 253: Sweep, Persefoni, Sprih
- Indian group facing EU and Indian rules: TSC NetZero, Carbon-OS, Intellocarbon
- Supplier answering a listed customer: Senseible, Intellocarbon, TSC NetZero

## How to choose

1. CSRD and ESRS reporting. Ask: "Does it run double materiality on the current CSRD scope?"
2. ISSB (IFRS S1 and S2). Ask: "Which IFRS S2 metrics come straight from our emissions data?"
3. SEBI BRSR and BRSR Core. Ask: "Does it produce BRSR Core with the evidence an assessor asks for?"
4. India’s Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (BEE emission intensity reports). Ask: "Does it track our intensity target and certificate position?"
5. California SB 253 and SB 261. Ask: "Which SB 253 outputs are included today?"
6. Built-in Scope 1, 2 and 3 calculation. Ask: "Are emissions calculated inside, or imported from another tool?"
7. Audit trail and assurance readiness: evidence, approvals, auditor access. Ask: "Can our auditor log in and trace a figure to its document?"
8. Digital tagging (XBRL or iXBRL) for filing. Ask: "Does it produce the tagged file, or do we tag it elsewhere?"
