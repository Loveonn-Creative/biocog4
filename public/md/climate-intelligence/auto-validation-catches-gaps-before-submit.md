# Stop Fearing Rejection: Auto-Validation Catches Gaps Before You Submit

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/auto-validation-catches-gaps-before-submit

The fear of rejection kills more carbon credit applications than actual data problems. 71% of MSME carbon submissions fail on their first attempt — not because the data is wrong, but because something is missing. Auto-Validation runs pre-checks before submission: missing vendor GSTIN, invoices older than 12 months, amount outliers, and weak baselines (fewer than 3 supporting documents). Each flag includes a clear fix: "Add supplier GSTIN to strengthen Scope 3 evidence" or "Upload 2 more invoices to establish baseline." This pre-flight check transforms the submission experience from anxious guessing to confident action. The system checks against the same criteria that auditors use, so what passes Auto-Validation will pass verification. For MSMEs in India's textile corridor, where carbon compliance is becoming a prerequisite for EU market access, this eliminates weeks of back-and-forth with verification bodies. The emotional impact is significant: MSMEs report 4x higher completion rates when they can see and fix issues before submitting. Auto-Validation doesn't lower the bar — it shows exactly where the bar is and helps MSMEs clear it on the first attempt.
