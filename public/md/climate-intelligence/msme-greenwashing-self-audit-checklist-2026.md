# A Greenwashing Self-Audit Checklist: 18 Tests Before You Make a Climate Claim

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/msme-greenwashing-self-audit-checklist-2026

EU Green Claims Directive (provisional 2026), India's CCPA Greenwashing Guidelines (2024), and FTC Green Guides (US) all sharpen enforcement. An MSME that publishes "we are carbon neutral" without backing data faces fines up to 4% of turnover in the EU.

## The 18 tests

### Evidence

1. Do you have a documented baseline year and methodology?
2. Is your baseline verified by an independent third party?
3. Are emission factors sourced from a recognised database with citation?
4. Are calculations reproducible from raw bills and invoices?

### Boundary

1. Have you stated which scopes are included (1, 2, 3)?
2. Have you disclosed Scope 3 categories included and excluded?
3. Have you included or explicitly excluded biogenic carbon?
4. Is the geographic and temporal boundary clear?

### Claim language

1. Avoid "carbon neutral" without offset disclosure
2. Avoid "100% green" without renewable certification reference
3. Avoid "net zero" before 2030 unless validated by SBTi
4. Use specific numbers, not vague adjectives ("low-carbon")

### Offsets

1. If using offsets, are they additional, permanent, verified?
2. Have you stated the % of footprint offset vs reduced?
3. Do offsets meet ICVCM Core Carbon Principles?

### Improvement

1. Is there a year-on-year reduction trajectory disclosed?
2. Is the trajectory aligned with 1.5°C (4.2% absolute/year)?
3. Is there a transition plan with capex and timeline?

## How regulators interpret each test

The EU Green Claims Directive treats absent evidence as misleading by default. The burden of proof is on the claimant.

## Score yourself

- 16–18: Defensible. Publish.
- 12–15: Defensible with refinement. Publish a more modest claim.
- 8–11: Risky. Limit to internal use.
- Under 8: Withdraw any external claim.
