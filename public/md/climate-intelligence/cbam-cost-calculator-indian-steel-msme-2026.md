# CBAM Cost Calculator for Indian Steel MSMEs: Free Tool

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/cbam-cost-calculator-indian-steel-msme-2026

Indian steel MSMEs exporting to the EU need to calculate CBAM exposure before 2026. The cost depends on emission intensity, EU ETS price (EUR 85/tonne), and CBAM phase-in percentage. Production route matters: BF-BOF 1.8-2.2 tCO2/t, EAF with scrap 0.4-0.8 tCO2/t, EAF with DRI 1.2-1.6 tCO2/t. Phase-in: 2026 2.5%, 2027 5%, 2028 10%, 2029 22.5%, 2030 48.5%, 2031 61%, 2032 73.5%, 2033 86%, 2034 100%. Example for 5,000 tonnes BF-BOF at 2.0 tCO2/t: 2026 CBAM cost EUR 21,250, 2034 CBAM cost EUR 850,000. India has no domestic carbon price currently deductible. Use Senseible free CBAM calculator to input actual production data and get year-by-year cost projections using India grid factor (0.708 kgCO2/kWh).
