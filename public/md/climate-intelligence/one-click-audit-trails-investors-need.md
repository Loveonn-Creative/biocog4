# One-Click Audit Trails: What Investors Actually Need

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/one-click-audit-trails-investors-need

Investors reviewing carbon assets need one thing: a complete, unbroken chain from source document to credit value. Most MSMEs cannot produce this without weeks of consultant work. One-Click Audit Trail Export generates an investor-ready XLSX with every row containing: document SHA256 hash, invoice date, vendor, emission category, activity data, emission factor with source, CO₂ calculation, verification score, compliance status, and proof chain notation. The export includes an Audit Summary sheet with methodology version, total entries, verified count, and a statement: "Every row traces from source invoice to carbon outcome." This format satisfies requirements for IEX Green Market listing, SIDBI green loan applications, and voluntary carbon credit buyers. A Pune auto-parts manufacturer recently used the audit trail to secure ₹12L in green financing — the bank's ESG team approved in 3 days instead of the typical 6 weeks because the documentation was already complete. The export uses BIOCOG_MVR_INDIA_v1.0 methodology versioning, so any future audit can reproduce the exact calculations. No rework, no reinterpretation — what the MSME sees is what the investor sees.
