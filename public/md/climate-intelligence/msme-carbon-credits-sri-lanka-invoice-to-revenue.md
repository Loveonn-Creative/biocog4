# MSME Carbon Credits in Sri Lanka: From Invoice to Revenue

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/msme-carbon-credits-sri-lanka-invoice-to-revenue

Published 2026-04-02.

Sri Lankan MSMEs in apparel, tea processing, and rubber manufacturing can monetize verified emission reductions as carbon credits. Credit prices in the voluntary carbon market vary widely by standard, project type and vintage. Use the latest official Sri Lanka grid emission factor for Scope 2. Revenue pathway: (1) Establish baseline using 12 months of invoice data, (2) Implement reduction measures (solar, efficiency, fuel switching), (3) Document reductions with before and after invoice data, (4) Register with carbon standard and issue credits, (5) List on voluntary market platforms. For example, an apparel manufacturer that replaces part of its grid electricity with rooftop solar can estimate the avoided emissions as solar kWh × the grid factor; what those credits are worth depends on the price a buyer will actually pay. CBSL sustainable finance taxonomy supports green lending for verified carbon reduction projects.
