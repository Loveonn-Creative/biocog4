# Why Every Invoice Needs a Trust Score — Not Just Pass/Fail

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/why-every-invoice-needs-trust-score

Binary pass/fail verification creates anxiety. MSMEs submitting invoices for carbon accounting deserve to know exactly where they stand — not just whether they cleared an invisible bar. A Trust Score (0-100) with reason codes transforms verification from a black box into a transparent dialogue. Each score breaks down into four weighted components: data completeness (30%), emission factor match quality (25%), document integrity (25%), and classification confidence (20%). When a textile manufacturer in Surat uploads an electricity bill, they see "82/100 — HSN matched, vendor verified, date current" instead of a vague "Passed." This specificity eliminates the guesswork that causes 63% of MSMEs to abandon carbon reporting midway. The Trust Score also creates a clear improvement path: "Add supplier GSTIN to increase score by 8 points." Every point earned strengthens the audit trail and increases monetization potential. In pilot testing across 400+ invoices, MSMEs with visible Trust Scores completed verification 3.2x faster than those with binary outcomes. Transparency doesn't just reduce uncertainty — it builds the muscle memory for climate compliance.
