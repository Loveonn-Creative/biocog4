# A 12-Month Net-Zero Roadmap for MSMEs With No Sustainability Team

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/msme-net-zero-roadmap-12-months-no-consultants

Published 2026-05-06.

Many MSME net-zero strategies stall because they are written by consultants who leave after the report. This roadmap assumes you have one operations person with two hours per week. For targets, renewable procurement and audits in one plan, see [decarbonisation strategy and net-zero roadmaps](https://senseible.earth/decarbonization).

## Month 1: Baseline

Pull last 12 months of electricity bills, fuel invoices, freight bills and biggest material POs. Upload them at [Senseible](https://senseible.earth/): the OCR extracts emissions automatically. Output: total tCO2e split by Scope 1, 2, 3.

## Month 2: Hot-spot map

Most emissions usually sit in a few line items. Identify yours. For most MSMEs it is electricity + freight + one raw material.

## Month 3: Set the SBTi-aligned target

1.5°C trajectory = 4.2% absolute reduction per year. For a 100 tCO2e baseline that is 4.2 tCO2e to remove in year 1.

| Year | Target % reduction | Cumulative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | 4.2 | 4.2 |
| 2027 | 4.2 | 8.4 |
| 2030 | 4.2 | 21.0 |
| 2035 | 4.2 | 42.0 |
| 2050 | 4.2 | 100 |

## Months 4–6: Easy wins

- LED retrofit (cuts lighting electricity, usually with a short payback)
- Compressed air leak audit (low cost, cuts compressor electricity)
- Procurement switch to nearest supplier (cuts freight emissions)

## Months 7–9: Capex decisions

Apply your [internal carbon price](https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/msme-internal-carbon-price-implementation-guide). Decide on rooftop solar, heat pump, or freight mode shift using the [Energy Transition calculator](https://senseible.earth/calculators/energy-transition-savings).

## Months 10–11: Engage suppliers

Send your top 5 suppliers a one-page PCF request. Use it to qualify them for renewals.

## Month 12: Disclose

Publish on your website: baseline, target, year-1 progress, year-2 plan. This helps with buyer queries, green loan applications and tender shortlists.

The roadmap is not the hard part. Consistency is. Block a recurring 2-hour calendar entry for the first Friday of each month.
