# Green Loan Eligibility for Pakistani Small Businesses: What Banks Need

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/green-loan-eligibility-pakistani-small-businesses

Published 2026-04-06.

State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Green Banking Guidelines encourage banks to expand lending to green projects. For SMEs, this can mean access to preferential financing for businesses with verified environmental data. Pakistani small businesses in textile, leather, and food processing sectors can seek green loans from banks participating in SBP refinancing schemes. The key requirement: documented carbon footprint and a credible reduction plan. Use the latest official Pakistan grid emission factor for Scope 2. Green loan application process: (1) Calculate your baseline carbon footprint using Senseible, (2) Identify reduction opportunities in energy efficiency, (3) Generate a 3-year reduction roadmap, (4) Submit to bank with NTN (tax ID) and verified emission data. World Bank and ADB also run climate finance programmes in Pakistan.
