# Honest Uncertainty: Why Confidence Bands Beat False Precision

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/confidence-bands-beat-false-precision

A carbon platform that says "your emissions are exactly 1,247.38 kg CO₂e" is lying. Every measurement has uncertainty — from OCR extraction accuracy to emission factor variance. Confidence Bands show the truth: "1,247 kg ± 5% (range: 1,185–1,310 kg)." This honesty builds more trust than false precision ever could. The band width is deterministic: HSN-matched classifications carry ±2% factor variance, keyword-based classifications ±8%, and generic classifications ±15%. OCR confidence adds another ±2-7% depending on document quality. Together, these create a probability envelope that auditors respect and investors trust. For an MSME applying for green loans, showing "87% ± 5% confidence" with a breakdown of uncertainty sources demonstrates scientific rigor that banks value. SBI's green finance team has noted that applications with explicit uncertainty ranges receive 30% faster approval because they signal data maturity. False precision, by contrast, triggers additional verification rounds. The climate science community has long embraced uncertainty quantification — IPCC reports famously use "likely (66-100%)" and "very likely (90-100%)" language. Carbon accounting for MSMEs should do the same.
