# See Your Carbon Revenue Before You Verify

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/see-carbon-revenue-before-verify

Abstract MRV numbers don't motivate action. But "If verified: ₹5,600–₹7,000 in carbon credits + ₹2,500 green loan savings" changes everything. Real-Time Monetization Preview converts every invoice's CO₂ output into tangible INR value before verification even begins. The calculation is transparent: CO₂ (kg) ÷ 1000 × ₹750/tCO₂e (current Indian voluntary market rate) = carbon credit value. Conservative estimate uses 80% multiplier; optimistic uses 100%. Green loan savings calculate at 0.5% interest reduction on ₹5L average MSME loan. These are not promises — they're evidence-based projections using published market rates. For a Tirupur textile manufacturer processing 50 electricity bills, seeing "potential quarterly value: ₹23,000–₹28,000" transforms carbon accounting from a compliance burden into a revenue opportunity. The preview updates in real time as each invoice is processed, creating a running total that builds motivation to complete the full submission. Early data shows MSMEs who see monetization previews complete 4.7x more invoice uploads per session. The psychological shift from "I have to do this" to "I want to do this" is the difference between compliance theater and genuine climate action.
