# Philippines Food Processing: Carbon Measurement Guide for Exporters

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/philippines-food-processing-carbon-measurement-guide

Published 2026-03-12.

The Philippines is a major food processing hub exporting canned goods, coconut products, and processed seafood. Use the latest official Philippines grid emission factor. Key emission sources: refrigeration and cold storage, cooking and sterilization, packaging, and wastewater treatment. DTI sustainability guidelines and BOI green incentives reward companies with environmental documentation. Scope breakdown for a canned goods processor: Scope 1 (diesel, LPG, refrigerants), Scope 2 (Meralco electricity), Scope 3 (raw materials, packaging, freight). Quick wins: (1) Replace R-22 refrigerants, (2) Solar rooftop, (3) Biogas from wastewater, (4) LED lighting and VFD installation. Filipino food processors with verified carbon data are better placed with sustainability-conscious EU retailers.
