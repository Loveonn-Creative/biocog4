# How Do You Compare? Dynamic Benchmarking for MSMEs

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/climate-intelligence/dynamic-benchmarking-400m-msmes

Knowing your Scope 1 emissions are 3,200 kg CO₂e means nothing without context. Are you efficient or wasteful? Dynamic Benchmarking answers this: "Your electricity intensity is 23% below textile sector average" or "Your Scope 1 ratio is 12% above manufacturing benchmark." These comparisons use sector-specific benchmark data, normalized by revenue and employee count. A pharmaceutical manufacturer in Hyderabad instantly sees how their emission profile compares to peers — not global corporations, but businesses of similar size and sector. This context transforms raw numbers into actionable intelligence. If you're 15% below sector average, that's a competitive advantage worth marketing to EU buyers under CBAM. If you're 20% above, the system suggests specific reduction strategies: "Switching to solar PPA would reduce your Scope 2 by 40% and bring you below sector median." Benchmarking also builds confidence for monetization: MSMEs below sector average qualify for premium carbon credit pricing. The data is updated as more MSMEs join the platform, creating a living benchmark that becomes more accurate over time. No consultant, no industry report — just your position relative to peers, updated in real time.
