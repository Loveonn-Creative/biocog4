When a carbon credit buyer asks "how was this CO₂ number calculated?" and the answer is "our AI model determined it," trust evaporates. Proof Graphs solve this by making every calculation step visible: Invoice → Line Item → Emission Factor → Scope Classification → CO₂ Value → Credit Eligibility.
