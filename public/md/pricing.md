# Pricing

Public verification and the five calculators are free forever. Paid plans unlock persistence, audit packs, and unlimited verifications.

## Plans

- **Free.** Public verification, five calculators, manual save, three calculator runs stored locally.
- **MSME Pro — ₹499 / month on yearly billing (₹1,999 monthly).** Unlimited verifications, autosave, CSV and JSON export, basic audit pack, single user.
- **MSME Business — ₹4,999 / month on yearly billing (₹9,999 monthly).** Everything in Pro, plus multi-user, full audit pack with SHA-256 evidence index, BRSR / GRI / TCFD export, priority support.
- **Enterprise.** Custom — includes SSO, dedicated environment, Cloud and SaaS fixed emission factors, audit ledger export.

## Billing logic

Launch prices (₹499 / ₹4,999) apply only on yearly billing. Monthly billing is offered at the standard rate (₹1,999 / ₹9,999). The checkout flow surfaces a one-tap upsell to switch to yearly before proceeding to Razorpay. Yearly subscriptions auto-set expiry to +12 months.

## Payments

Indian payments are processed through Razorpay. GST-compliant invoices are generated automatically post-payment and stored in a private bucket; signed download URLs are valid for one year.

## Refunds

See the [refund policy](/legal/refund). Pro-rated refunds are available within seven days of first payment of a new annual cycle.
