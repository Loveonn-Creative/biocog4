# Digital MRV platforms, compared

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/best-digital-mrv-platforms

Digital MRV is meant to shorten the months between monitoring and issuance. Some tools here measure land from space, one turns a registry methodology into a workflow, and two are pilots run by the registries themselves. Pick what you need to prove.

Senseible makes one of the tools on this list. Checked 29 October 2026.

## The list

### 1. Hedera Guardian

- Who it is for: Registries, project developers and builders who want to digitise a methodology into a dMRV workflow.
- Best for: Registries and developers building their own digitised methodology workflows.
- Pricing: Free open-source software (Apache 2.0); hosting and Hedera network fees are the user's.
- Strengths: Open source with methodology templates for many registries. Public-ledger record of each workflow step.
- Limits: A toolkit you deploy and configure, not a managed measurement service. No remote sensing of its own.

### 2. Sylithe

- Who it is for: Project developers and credit buyers; nature-based projects (ARR, REDD+, agroforestry) and biochar.
- Best for: Indian ARR, REDD+ and agroforestry developers preparing for the Indian Carbon Market.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: India-specific forest models and Indian Carbon Market alignment. Dynamic baselines with matched control areas.
- Limits: No independent validation found. Nature-based and biochar only.

### 3. PramaanOne

- Who it is for: Enterprises needing emissions evidence; also project originators and registries (site text).
- Best for: Indian enterprises exploring meter- and satellite-backed emissions evidence.
- Pricing: Not published.
- Strengths: Covers enterprise emissions evidence and project credits in one product. Meter, IoT and satellite inputs.
- Limits: Little public detail on how it works. No pricing, customers or independent approvals found.

### 4. Boomitra

- Who it is for: Credit buyers and governments; farmers and ranchers across the Global South.
- Best for: Buyers seeking soil-carbon removal credits measured by satellite.
- Pricing: Not applicable (sells credits).
- Strengths: Remote sensing calibrated on a very large soil-sample archive. VM0042 alignment stated.
- Limits: Not software other developers can license. Soil carbon only.

### 5. Verra digital MRV (Project Hub DMRV pilots)

- Who it is for: VCS project proponents and VVBs.
- Best for: VCS developers who want faster, more frequent issuance on supported methodologies.
- Pricing: Registry fees apply.
- Strengths: The registry itself is running DMRV pilots, so outputs land where credits are issued. Vets third-party data providers for ARR.
- Limits: Pilot stage; limited methodologies. Not a measurement tool; developers still need a data provider.

### 6. Gold Standard dMRV pilot programme

- Who it is for: GS4GG project developers and verifiers.
- Best for: Gold Standard developers testing digital monitoring with the registry.
- Strengths: Registry-backed route to digital verification. Open to developer-proposed tools.
- Limits: Pilot with an October 2026 end date. Not software; the developer supplies the tool.

### 7. Senseible

- Who it is for: MSMEs, exporters and enterprises proving their own Scope 1, 2 and 3 figures to lenders, buyers and auditors.
- Best for: Companies that need verifiable emissions evidence for lenders or buyers, not carbon project developers.
- Pricing: Free Snapshot plan. Paid plans from ₹499 a month billed yearly, plus GST. Scale on request.
- Strengths: Document-level fingerprints and factor sources for company emissions evidence. Low entry price for small firms.
- Limits: Not a crediting dMRV: no registry methodology, issuance or project monitoring. Verification is Senseible's own expert-supervised check, not independent assurance.

## Which tools fit which situation

- Forest, farm or soil project developer: Sylithe, Boomitra, PramaanOne
- Building our own methodology workflow: Hedera Guardian, Sylithe, Verra digital MRV (Project Hub DMRV pilots)
- Credit buyer checking quality: Sylithe, Boomitra, PramaanOne
- Project aiming at the Indian Carbon Market: Sylithe, Boomitra, Verra digital MRV (Project Hub DMRV pilots)
- Company proving its own emissions to lenders or buyers: PramaanOne, Senseible, Hedera Guardian

## How to choose

1. Satellite or remote-sensing measurement (biomass, soil carbon, land use). Ask: "Which satellites, at what resolution, and how was the model calibrated?"
2. Field samples, plots, meters or IoT feeds tied into the model. Ask: "How do field samples or meter readings correct the model?"
3. Built to a registry methodology (Verra, Gold Standard, Indian Carbon Market or others). Ask: "Which methodology and version, and has the registry accepted it?"
4. Independent quality or risk rating of credits or projects. Ask: "Who rates the risk, and are they independent of the seller?"
5. Tamper-evident record: hashing, ledger or versioned audit trail. Ask: "If a reading changes, where does the old value show?"
6. Supports a verification body (VVB) review or digital issuance. Ask: "Can our verifier work inside it, or do we send files?"
7. Tools for developers to run monitoring and produce issuance-ready reports. Ask: "Can we run monitoring ourselves, or only buy it as a service?"
