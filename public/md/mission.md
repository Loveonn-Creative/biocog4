# Mission

Carbon verification today fails at the system level, not the individual level. A bank cannot underwrite a green loan it cannot trust. A buyer cannot pay a premium for a credit it cannot trace. A regulator cannot enforce a disclosure it cannot reproduce. The result is a stuck market — and 400 million MSMEs locked out of climate finance because the cost of proving they are clean is higher than the value of being clean.

Senseible exists to remove that gap. The mission is a verification primitive cheap enough and fast enough that proving an emission becomes a default operation — like signing a PDF — instead of a quarterly project. When the cost of proof collapses, finance, regulation and credit markets unlock together.

The metric we hold ourselves to: document to verified outcome in under 47 seconds, first response in under two seconds, and zero successful replay of the same evidence twice.
