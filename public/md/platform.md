# The Senseible platform

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/platform

Senseible is a verification layer for climate data. It turns a business document, such as an invoice, an energy bill or a freight slip, into an emission record that shows its source document, emission factor and methodology version.

## Who it's for

- MSMEs and exporters: turn invoices into a verified emission record without a consultant.
- Enterprises: collect Scope 3 supplier data for CBAM, ISSB and CSRD reporting.
- Banks and lenders: underwrite against figures tied to hashed source documents, not borrower self-declarations.
- Carbon credit buyers: check evidence linkage behind MSME-origin credits.
- Ecosystem partners: plug verified MSME climate data into your own product.

## How it works

1. Capture: drop a document, take a photo or speak the data point.
2. Verify: every document is hashed, parsed, classified deterministically and matched against country grid factors.
3. Use: the verified record feeds your disclosure, your lender, your reduction roadmap and, where eligible, your credits.

## Outcomes

- [Report](https://senseible.earth/trust): one ledger for CBAM, BRSR, GHG Protocol, ISSB, CSRD, TCFD, GRI and SBTi disclosures.
- [Finance](https://senseible.earth/climate-finance): verified baselines become underwriting inputs.
- [Decarbonise](https://senseible.earth/net-zero): a baseline becomes a sector-aware reduction roadmap.
- [Monetise](https://senseible.earth/carbon-credits): records that clear additionality become credit-readiness signals.
