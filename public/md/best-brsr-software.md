# BRSR software, compared

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/best-brsr-software

SEBI changed BRSR Core in March 2025: assessment or assurance, and value chain disclosure made voluntary from FY 2025-26. Some tools still describe the old rules. Pick where you sit and the list re-orders by fit.

Senseible makes one of the tools on this list. Checked 29 October 2026.

## The list

### 1. TSC NetZero for BRSR and BRSR Core

- Who it is for: Top 1,000 listed companies and their value chains.
- Best for: Top 250 listed companies collecting value chain data and filing XBRL.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: End-to-end: value chain collection, audit trail and XBRL in one tool. Quarterly or half-yearly drafts roll up into the annual report.
- Limits: No published BRSR prices. Its pages do not mention SEBI's March 2025 changes.

### 2. CarbonNeeti

- Who it is for: Indian manufacturers; advisors and verifiers as free seats.
- Best for: Listed manufacturers in the 251 to 1,000 band preparing BRSR Core and CCTS from plant bills.
- Pricing: BRSR: ₹50,000 a month plus 18% GST (one company, up to 5 plants); BRSR + CCTS: ₹1,00,000 a month plus GST; Group: ₹3,75,000 a month plus GST (up to 15 plants); Enterprise on request. Yearly billing charges 10 months for 12.
- Strengths: Each figure tied to the bill page, factor edition and two approvers. Published prices and a 30-day trial.
- Limits: Value chain collection not described. A young product (started January 2025); data hosted outside India.

### 3. CarbonLens

- Who it is for: Mid-market listed companies ranked 251 to 1,000; CA firms.
- Best for: Mid-cap listed companies wanting a priced, self-serve BRSR tool, after checking its rule statements.
- Pricing: Starter ₹2,49,000 a year (Scope 1 and 2, BRSR Core, up to 3 users); Growth ₹4,99,000 a year (Scope 3, Tally, bill reading, CCTS, up to 10 users); Enterprise custom.
- Strengths: Published annual prices and Tally integration. Portal for CA firms reviewing several clients.
- Limits: Regulatory timeline on its pages is out of date on assurance and value chain. XBRL output not stated.

### 4. Carbon-OS (BRSR Core)

- Who it is for: Listed companies, especially those entering BRSR Core assessment or assurance.
- Best for: Companies in the top 1,000 facing their first BRSR Core assessment or assurance.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Rule statements match SEBI's current framework. Each BRSR Core cell clicks through to its records.
- Limits: XBRL output not stated. No published prices.

### 5. Sprih

- Who it is for: Large enterprises and value chains.
- Best for: Listed companies that want last year's filing checked before choosing a tool.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Free check of last year's BRSR (PDF against XBRL, totals, intensities). Value chain module.
- Limits: BRSR Core workflow not described. No prices.

### 6. Senseible

- Who it is for: Unlisted suppliers asked for figures by a listed customer, and listed companies needing Scope 1, Scope 2 and energy figures traced to bills.
- Best for: Suppliers and smaller listed companies that need defensible Scope 1, 2 and energy figures for BRSR.
- Pricing: Free Snapshot plan. Paid plans from ₹499 a month billed yearly, plus GST. Scale on request.
- Strengths: Each energy and emissions figure traces to a specific bill and factor. Free plan and low published prices, suited to suppliers asked for data.
- Limits: Covers two of nine BRSR Core attributes; not a full BRSR tool. Draft is self-declared; no XBRL output.

### 7. Sustaineve

- Who it is for: Mid-size Indian manufacturers, priced by facility count.
- Best for: Manufacturers needing Principle 6 figures now and willing to wait for the rest.
- Pricing: Essential ₹1.5 lakh to ₹3 lakh a year (1 to 3 facilities); Growth ₹4 lakh to ₹6 lakh a year (3 to 6 facilities); Enterprise ₹8 lakh to ₹12 lakh a year, custom contract (6 to 15 facilities). Billed annually.
- Strengths: Unlimited users, priced per facility with published ranges. Manufacturing-specific Scope 1 categories with state grid factors.
- Limits: Social and Governance modules, needed for full BRSR, not yet live when checked. Scope 3 marked coming soon.

### 8. Intellocarbon ESG Disclosure Bundle

- Who it is for: Indian industrial MSMEs and listed entities.
- Best for: Exporters that need BRSR Core alongside CBAM and CCTS from one vendor.
- Pricing: Not published.
- Strengths: One subscription also covers ISSB, GRI, CSRD and CDP. Records the company's cohort and states whether assurance is due.
- Limits: Assurance wording out of date. No value chain or XBRL detail.

### 9. Updapt BRSR reporting

- Who it is for: Indian enterprises.
- Best for: Enterprises wanting a broad ESG data tool that includes BRSR.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Free trial. Supplier assessment and double materiality modules alongside BRSR.
- Limits: Little BRSR detail is published on its site. No BRSR Core or XBRL detail found.

### 10. Karbon (BRSR)

- Who it is for: Listed and public-sector enterprises in hard-to-abate sectors.
- Best for: PSUs and infrastructure companies that want BRSR delivered as a service.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Shows ESG rating score changes for named listed clients (vendor's data). Consulting and assurance support.
- Limits: BRSR functions described only at headline level. No prices.

### 11. Greenalign

- Who it is for: Manufacturers and exporters.
- Best for: Manufacturers that want reduction projects bundled with reporting.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Reporting plus a marketplace of reduction services. Gap guidance on missing data.
- Limits: Little BRSR detail published. No prices.

## Which tools fit which situation

- Top 250 listed company: TSC NetZero for BRSR and BRSR Core, CarbonNeeti, CarbonLens
- Listed 251 to 1,000, first BRSR Core check: CarbonNeeti, Carbon-OS (BRSR Core), TSC NetZero for BRSR and BRSR Core
- Supplier asked for figures by a listed customer: CarbonNeeti, Senseible, CarbonLens
- Manufacturer working from plant bills: CarbonNeeti, Carbon-OS (BRSR Core), Senseible

## How to choose

1. Full BRSR in SEBI format: all nine principles, essential and leadership indicators. Ask: "Does it produce all nine principles, or only the environment section?"
2. BRSR Core: the nine attributes, with evidence ready for assessment or assurance. Ask: "For each Core attribute, what evidence sits behind the number?"
3. Collects data from value chain partners (2% or more of purchases or sales). Ask: "How do our partners send data, and what does it cost them?"
4. Scope 1 and 2 from activity data with Indian factors (CEA grid factor by year). Ask: "Which CEA grid factor version does it use for this financial year?"
5. Source document, factor and approver kept against each figure. Ask: "Click a figure: who approved it, and from which bill?"
6. Exchange XBRL output for filing. Ask: "Does it produce the exchange XBRL file?"
7. Reflects SEBI’s March 2025 changes. Ask: "Show us where it handles assessment versus assurance."
8. Price published on its site. Ask: "What will year one cost, all in?"
