# Carbon Accounting by Industry

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/industries

Carbon accounting for steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, textiles, chemicals, ceramics, paper, food, logistics, construction and auto parts.

- [Steel Production](https://senseible.earth/industries/steel): What are the embedded emissions per tonne of the steel you ship to us? (covered by EU CBAM)
- [Aluminium](https://senseible.earth/industries/aluminium): Can you give us actual embedded emissions for these extrusions, and how much is recycled metal? (covered by EU CBAM)
- [Cement and Clinker](https://senseible.earth/industries/cement): What is the embedded carbon per tonne of this cement, and what is its clinker content? (covered by EU CBAM)
- [Fertilisers](https://senseible.earth/industries/fertiliser): What are the embedded emissions per tonne of this fertiliser, including nitrous oxide? (covered by EU CBAM)
- [Textile Manufacturing](https://senseible.earth/industries/textile): Please share your factory’s energy use and emissions for last year.
- [Chemicals](https://senseible.earth/industries/chemical): Can you report Scope 1 and 2 for the site that makes our product?
- [Ceramics and Tiles](https://senseible.earth/industries/ceramics): What is the carbon footprint per square metre of these tiles?
- [Paper and Packaging](https://senseible.earth/industries/paper): What are the emissions per tonne of the board you supply us?
- [Food Processing](https://senseible.earth/industries/food-processing): Please report your site emissions for our supplier programme.
- [Logistics and Transport](https://senseible.earth/industries/logistics): What are the emissions for the consignments you moved for us this quarter?
- [Construction and Real Estate](https://senseible.earth/industries/construction): What are the site emissions and the embodied carbon of materials on this project?
- [Automotive Components](https://senseible.earth/industries/automobile): Please submit your plant’s Scope 1 and 2 and a footprint for the parts you supply.
