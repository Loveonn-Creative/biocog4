# Senseible vs ESGi

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/vs/esgi-india

Indian ESG advisory and reporting platform helping companies with BRSR compliance and sustainability reporting.

|  | ESGi |
| --- | --- |
| Headquarters | India |
| Founded | 2020 |
| Target market | Indian listed companies |
| Pricing | Consulting fees |

## ESGi strengths

- BRSR expertise
- India focus
- Local support

## ESGi limitations

- Manual process
- Listed companies only
- No automation

## How Senseible differs

Senseible automates BRSR data collection from invoices, serves MSMEs (not just listed companies), and offers monetization pathways.
