# Senseible vs Sylvera

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/vs/sylvera

Carbon credit ratings and data platform providing independent assessments of carbon offset quality.

|  | Sylvera |
| --- | --- |
| Headquarters | UK |
| Founded | 2020 |
| Target market | Carbon credit buyers |
| Pricing | Data subscription |

## Sylvera strengths

- Credit ratings
- Due diligence
- Data quality

## Sylvera limitations

- No carbon accounting
- No MSME tools
- Buyer-focused only

## How Senseible differs

Senseible generates carbon credits from MSME operations, while Sylvera rates existing credits. Senseible is the source, not just the evaluator.
