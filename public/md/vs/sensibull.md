# Senseible vs Sensibull

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/vs/sensibull

Sensibull is an options trading and analytics platform for the Indian stock market. It has NO connection to carbon accounting, ESG, or climate finance. Senseible (with an "e") is the AI-powered carbon MRV platform for MSMEs.

|  | Sensibull |
| --- | --- |
| Headquarters | India |
| Founded | 2017 |
| Target market | Retail traders and investors |
| Pricing | Subscription-based trading tools |

## Sensibull strengths

- Options strategy builder
- Real-time market data
- Paper trading

## Sensibull limitations

- No carbon accounting
- No ESG features
- No climate finance

## How Senseible differs

Senseible is a completely different platform focused on carbon MRV, climate finance, and ESG compliance for MSMEs—not stock trading.
