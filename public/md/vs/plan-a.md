# Senseible vs Plan A

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/vs/plan-a

German carbon accounting and net zero planning platform for European companies with decarbonization roadmaps.

|  | Plan A |
| --- | --- |

| Target market | European SMEs and enterprises |

## Plan A strengths

- EU compliance
- Net zero planning
- SME focus

## Plan A limitations

- No India presence
- No invoice automation
- No monetization

## How Senseible differs

Senseible automates carbon from invoices in seconds, offers monetization to credits/loans, and bridges India-EU compliance requirements.
