# Senseible vs Verra

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/vs/verra

The Verified Carbon Standard (VCS) is the world's most widely used voluntary carbon credit certification program.

|  | Verra |
| --- | --- |
| Headquarters | USA |
| Founded | 2007 |
| Target market | Carbon project developers |
| Pricing | Registration fees |

## Verra strengths

- Global standard
- Market recognition
- Methodology library

## Verra limitations

- Long certification
- High cost
- Project-based only

## How Senseible differs

Senseible automates MRV that can feed into Verra projects, making verification faster and cheaper for MSMEs seeking certification.
