# Senseible vs Zeroboard

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/vs/zeroboard

Japanese carbon accounting platform for supply chain emissions with strong presence in Asia-Pacific markets.

|  | Zeroboard |
| --- | --- |
| Headquarters | Japan |
| Founded | 2021 |
| Target market | Japanese enterprises and supply chains |
| Pricing | $30,000+ annually |

## Zeroboard strengths

- Asia presence
- Supply chain
- Japanese compliance

## Zeroboard limitations

- No India focus
- No invoice automation
- Enterprise pricing

## How Senseible differs

Senseible is built India-first with GST integration, MSME pricing, and CBAM + BRSR compliance—not Japan-centric.
