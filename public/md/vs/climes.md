# Senseible vs Climes

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/vs/climes

Indian climate action platform focusing on carbon offsetting and sustainability for consumers and businesses.

|  | Climes |
| --- | --- |
| Headquarters | India |
| Founded | 2020 |
| Target market | Consumers and businesses |
| Pricing | Per-offset pricing |

## Climes strengths

- Consumer focus
- Offset marketplace
- India projects

## Climes limitations

- No MRV
- No carbon accounting
- Offset buyer only

## How Senseible differs

Senseible helps MSMEs generate verified carbon data that can become credits, while Climes helps buy offsets. Senseible is for earning, not just buying.
