# Senseible vs Updapt ESG

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/vs/updapt-esg

Indian ESG reporting and sustainability management platform for enterprises with BRSR and CDP support.

|  | Updapt ESG |
| --- | --- |
| Headquarters | India |
| Founded | 2019 |
| Target market | Indian enterprises |
| Pricing | Enterprise pricing |

## Updapt ESG strengths

- BRSR templates
- CDP support
- India presence

## Updapt ESG limitations

- Manual data entry
- No invoice automation
- No MSME tier

## How Senseible differs

Senseible automates carbon calculation from GST invoices, offers MSME pricing from free, and includes monetization to credits/loans.
