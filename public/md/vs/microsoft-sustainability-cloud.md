## Microsoft Sustainability Cloud strengths

- Enterprise integration
- Azure ecosystem
- Global support

## Microsoft Sustainability Cloud limitations

- Complex setup
- High cost
- Requires IT team
- No MSME focus

## How Senseible differs

Senseible offers instant carbon accounting from invoices without enterprise IT setup, at 1% of the cost, specifically designed for MSMEs in emerging markets.
