# Supplier emissions software, compared

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/best-supplier-emissions-software

Your Scope 3 sits with suppliers, and most of them will never fill a carbon questionnaire. These tools differ on what they ask suppliers for, who pays, and how honestly they label estimates. Pick your supplier base and the list re-orders by fit.

Senseible makes one of the tools on this list. Checked 29 October 2026.

## The list

### 1. Sweep (supply chain emissions)

- Who it is for: Enterprises with large supplier bases.
- Best for: Enterprises running a large, multi-country supplier programme.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Free supplier accounts with their own footprint dashboard. Imports existing CDP and EcoVadis data.
- Limits: No published pricing. Built for large programmes; no small-buyer plan found.

### 2. EcoVadis Carbon Action Manager

- Who it is for: Procurement and sustainability teams at large buyers with big supplier bases.
- Best for: Large buyers whose suppliers are already in the EcoVadis network.
- Pricing: Buyer side: on request. Supplier rating subscriptions are paid; prices not published.
- Strengths: Large existing network of suppliers already rated and reporting. Scorecards and corrective action plans for supplier improvement.
- Limits: Suppliers generally pay for EcoVadis subscriptions. Carbon scorecard only for companies sized S, M and L, per its pricing page.

### 3. carbmee EIS

- Who it is for: Enterprise manufacturers with complex supply chains.
- Best for: Large manufacturers that want ERP-driven Scope 3 with supplier engagement.
- Pricing: On request (Small, Medium, Large and Enterprise tiers, all quoted).
- Strengths: Transaction-level Scope 3 from ERP data. CBAM, PCF and corporate footprint modules alongside supplier engagement.
- Limits: No published prices. Enterprise focus; dedicated expert onboarding over about three months.

### 4. osapiens HUB (Supplier Intelligence and CCF)

- Who it is for: Companies managing supplier compliance (LkSG, CSDDD, EUDR) alongside carbon.
- Best for: European buyers combining Scope 3 with supply chain due diligence laws.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: One supplier record shared across carbon, CBAM and due diligence. Method and quality labelled per line for auditors.
- Limits: Supplier decarbonisation programme features not found. No published pricing.

### 5. The Sustainability Cloud (Scope 3 hub)

- Who it is for: Enterprises; India and other markets.
- Best for: Indian enterprises that want PACT-based supplier exchange with BRSR and CBAM in one suite.
- Pricing: Scope 3 is on higher plans; enterprise pricing is custom. Published SME plans cover Scope 1 and 2 only.
- Strengths: PACT-conformant exchange and data quality ratings. Rupee-priced entry plans.
- Limits: Scope 3 not in published SME plans. Supplier reduction programmes not found.

### 6. Senseible (enterprise supplier emissions)

- Who it is for: Enterprises sourcing from MSMEs, especially in India; exporters needing supplier data for CBAM and BRSR Core.
- Best for: Buyers whose Scope 3 sits with Indian MSME suppliers who keep bills but cannot fill questionnaires.
- Pricing: On request: priced by suppliers added, team seats and integrations. Pilots start with 10 to 15 suppliers.
- Strengths: Small suppliers send bills, not questionnaires; buyers get figures and proof hashes, not invoices. Labels every supplier line by method, from document-backed to spend-only.
- Limits: Supplier portal, ERP feeds and roll-up are built per enterprise, not self-serve today. No SOC 2 or ISO 27001; not an assurance provider.

### 7. Makersite (with SiGREEN, now Mattermaps)

- Who it is for: Manufacturers: product engineering, sustainability and procurement teams.
- Best for: Manufacturers in chemicals, automotive and industrials that need supplier product footprints.
- Pricing: On request ('custom price' on Pro, Teams and Enterprise). Annual licences paid upfront; Pro 1 user and 2,000 material components; Teams up to 10 users and 4,000; Enterprise 50,000.
- Strengths: Product-level supplier data with TfS, Catena-X and PACT exchange. Combines BoM, ERP and supplier data.
- Limits: Product-centric; heavy for companies that only need a corporate Scope 3 figure. No published price.

### 8. Watershed Supply Chain

- Who it is for: Large companies.
- Best for: Watershed customers extending into supplier engagement.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Supplier data flows directly into the corporate footprint. Tens of thousands of supplier disclosures already matched.
- Limits: No published pricing. Whether suppliers pay is not stated.

### 9. Persefoni Scope 3 Data Exchange

- Who it is for: Buyers asking suppliers for footprints; suppliers with no prior experience.
- Best for: Buyers whose suppliers have never measured and need a free tool to start.
- Pricing: Free for suppliers (Pro). Buyers sending requests need Advanced: on request.
- Strengths: Suppliers get a full free carbon accounting tool, not a survey. Copilot and Academy help first-time suppliers.
- Limits: Supplier figures are self-calculated; quality scoring not found. Reduction tracking with suppliers not found.

### 10. Sphera Supplier Engagement and Supplier PCF Calculator

- Who it is for: Procurement teams at industrial companies.
- Best for: Industrial buyers that want product-level Category 1 data from suppliers.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Free supplier PCF calculator backed by LCA data. Feeds supplier responses into risk and compliance views.
- Limits: Corporate Scope 3 spend fallback not described. No published pricing.

### 11. Sprih Supplier Data Exchange

- Who it is for: Enterprises with suppliers of mixed digital maturity.
- Best for: Buyers whose suppliers send documents rather than filled questionnaires.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Accepts supplier documents in any format, including utility bills. Audit trail of extraction per data point.
- Limits: Prioritisation and spend fallback not described. No published pricing.

### 12. CDP Supply Chain Membership

- Who it is for: Large buyers requesting environmental disclosure from suppliers.
- Best for: Large buyers that want standardised, comparable supplier disclosure.
- Pricing: On request.
- Strengths: Standardised, widely recognised disclosure format. Supplier answers can include emissions allocated to the requesting customer.
- Limits: An annual questionnaire cycle, not continuous data. Not a calculation tool; buyers still need carbon accounting software.

### 13. Salesforce Net Zero Cloud (External Engagement Management)

- Who it is for: Salesforce customers.
- Best for: Salesforce shops that want supplier ESG data in their CRM.
- Pricing: External Engagement Management add-on: US$60K per org a year, billed annually, on top of Net Zero Cloud (on request).
- Strengths: Published add-on price. Supplier data in the same system as CRM accounts.
- Limits: Expensive add-on; base edition price not published. Supplier features thin on the pricing page.

## Which tools fit which situation

- Buyer with Indian MSME suppliers: Senseible (enterprise supplier emissions), Sweep (supply chain emissions), The Sustainability Cloud (Scope 3 hub)
- Suppliers who send bills, not questionnaires: Senseible (enterprise supplier emissions), Sprih Supplier Data Exchange, Sweep (supply chain emissions)
- Large multi-country supplier programme: Sweep (supply chain emissions), EcoVadis Carbon Action Manager, carbmee EIS
- Product footprints from suppliers: The Sustainability Cloud (Scope 3 hub), Makersite (with SiGREEN, now Mattermaps), Sphera Supplier Engagement and Supplier PCF Calculator
- Cutting supplier emissions, not only counting: Sweep (supply chain emissions), EcoVadis Carbon Action Manager, carbmee EIS

## How to choose

1. Sends data requests to suppliers through a portal or campaign, with reminders. Ask: "How does a supplier with no login respond, and who chases them?"
2. Takes supplier-specific data: activity data, a company footprint or a product footprint. Ask: "What exactly does a supplier send: bills, a questionnaire or a footprint?"
3. Spend-based or average-data estimates for the long tail, labelled by method. Ask: "Can I see which lines are measured and which are spend estimates?"
4. Ranks suppliers and categories by emissions, so you know whom to ask first. Ask: "Which 20 suppliers should we start with, and why?"
5. A data-quality score or tier per supplier or data point. Ask: "How is each supplier’s data graded, and can our auditor see the grade?"
6. Joint targets, action plans or progress tracking with suppliers. Ask: "Can we set a target with a supplier and track it?"
7. Pulls purchase data from ERP or procurement systems; supports PACT or Catena-X. Ask: "Which ERP do you connect to today, without a custom project?"
8. Suppliers can respond without paying. Ask: "Does any supplier pay anything to respond?"
