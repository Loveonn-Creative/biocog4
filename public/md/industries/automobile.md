# Automotive Components: Carbon Accounting Guide

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/industries/automobile

Vehicle makers ask component suppliers for emissions to report their own supply chain. Casting, forging, paint shops and purchased steel and aluminium drive the number.

## The question vehicle maker (oem)s ask

“Please submit your plant’s Scope 1 and 2 and a footprint for the parts you supply.”

This sector is not directly covered by EU CBAM. Buyers, lenders and OEMs ask for emissions built from documents for their own reporting.

## Scope 1 sources (evidence: fuel invoices)

- Forging and heat-treatment furnaces
- Paint shop ovens
- Generators
- On-site vehicles

## Scope 2 sources (evidence: electricity bills)

- Machining centres
- Assembly lines
- Compressed air
- Paint booth ventilation

## Scope 3 sources (evidence: purchase and freight invoices)

- Steel and aluminium
- Castings and forgings bought in
- Plastics and rubber
- Freight to OEM

## Worked example

Illustrative monthly quantities: 1,50,000 kWh of grid electricity, 20,000 scm of piped natural gas, 2,000 litre of diesel, multiplied by the emission factors Senseible uses and annualised. Plant energy only. A part footprint also needs purchased material weights.

## Where reductions usually start

- Renewable electricity
- Furnace efficiency and heat recovery
- Compressed air leak repair
- Supplier engagement on steel and aluminium
