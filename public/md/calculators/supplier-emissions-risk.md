# Supplier emissions and Scope 3 risk calculator

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/calculators/supplier-emissions-risk

Score suppliers on Scope 3 Category 1 (purchased goods and services) emissions using a hybrid of spend-based and activity-based methods, aligned with the GHG Protocol Scope 3 Standard.

1. Add suppliers with spend, country and sector.
2. The calculator applies sector factors and country grid intensity.
3. Get tCO2e per supplier and a portfolio risk score with a confidence rating.
