# Carbon pricing impact calculator: EU ETS and CBAM

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/calculators/carbon-pricing-impact

Estimate carbon cost exposure under the EU ETS and CBAM from 2026 to 2034, including the CBAM phase-in, free allocation phase-out and best and worst case carbon price scenarios.

Free allocation still granted to EU producers, which sets the CBAM adjustment: 97.5% of the benchmark in 2026, falling to 51.5% in 2030 and 0% from 2034.
