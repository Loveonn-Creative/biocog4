# Product carbon footprint calculator (ISO 14067)

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/calculators/product-carbon-footprint

Calculate cradle-to-gate emissions per unit of product. Inputs are raw materials, energy, transport and packaging; the output is kgCO2e per unit with a line-by-line breakdown, following ISO 14067 and the GHG Protocol Product Standard.
