# About Senseible

Senseible is operated by Biocog Technologies, a Gurugram-based climate infrastructure company. The product is built for intelligent peers — operators, regulators, lenders and engineers — not for buyers of slogans.

## Why it exists

400 million MSMEs across India and emerging Asia are now indirectly inside the EU's carbon perimeter through CBAM, CSDDD and buyer-driven Scope 3 disclosure. None of them have access to consultant-grade MRV. The market built tools for the Fortune 500. Senseible builds infrastructure for everyone else.

## What is different

- Deterministic where it matters (HSN → scope, grid factor → kgCO2e), AI only where AI is honest (OCR / data extraction).
- Failures are explicit, with the exact math reason.
- Every verification carries a SHA-256 evidence hash that cannot be replayed.
- Private by default. RLS-enforced. IP addresses are hashed before logging.
