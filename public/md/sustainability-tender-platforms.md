# Sustainability tender platforms, compared

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/sustainability-tender-platforms

EU tender notices can carry green procurement criteria. UK central government contracts above £5 million a year ask bidders for a Carbon Reduction Plan. These are the official, free places to find those tenders. Pick where you want to bid.

Senseible is not a tender platform and is not on this list. Checked 29 October 2026.

## The list

### 1. TED (Tenders Electronic Daily)

- Who it is for: Suppliers bidding for EU public contracts; exporters and consultants.
- Best for: Exporters and consultants tracking EU public tenders with green criteria.
- Cost: Free.
- Strengths: Open API with bulk download. Notices carry structured green procurement fields.
- Limits: Green flag depends on the buyer filling it. EU only.

### 2. Find a Tender (UK)

- Who it is for: Suppliers bidding for UK public contracts.
- Best for: Suppliers to UK central government who must submit a Carbon Reduction Plan.
- Cost: Free.
- Strengths: One place for UK notices across the contract life cycle. Clear carbon rule for large central government contracts.
- Limits: No green filter found. UK only.

### 3. Contracts Finder (UK)

- Who it is for: Suppliers to England and non-devolved public bodies, including lower-value contracts.
- Best for: Small suppliers looking for lower-value UK public contracts.
- Cost: Free.
- Strengths: Lower-value contracts that smaller suppliers can win. Free, open licence.
- Limits: No sustainability filter. Mostly England.

## Which tools fit which situation

- Indian exporter eyeing EU tenders: TED (Tenders Electronic Daily), Find a Tender (UK), Contracts Finder (UK)
- Supplier to UK central government: Find a Tender (UK), Contracts Finder (UK), TED (Tenders Electronic Daily)
- Small supplier, lower-value contracts: Contracts Finder (UK), TED (Tenders Electronic Daily), Find a Tender (UK)

## How to choose

1. Whose tenders it covers. Ask: "Which buyers publish here, and above what value?"
2. Green or sustainability fields or filters on notices. Ask: "Can we find notices with green criteria without reading each one?"
3. Free to search and read notices. Ask: "Is anything behind a paywall?"
4. Saved searches or email alerts. Ask: "Can we get new notices by email?"
5. API or bulk data download. Ask: "Can we pull notices into our own sheet or system?"
6. Indian firms can read notices and bid where the tender allows. Ask: "Does this tender accept bidders from outside the country?"
7. Where verified emission evidence helps a bid. Ask: "What carbon evidence does this buyer score, and how?"
