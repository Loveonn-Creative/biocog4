# Privacy Policy

Canonical: https://senseible.earth/legal/privacy

Effective 27 August 2024. Last updated 16 October 2026.

## 0. Introduction & Legal Identity

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") governs the collection, use, processing, storage, transfer, and protection of information by: INSPYR FINNOVATION PRIVATE LIMITED, operating under the brand name "Senseible" ("Senseible", "Company", "We", "Us").

This Policy applies to all users, customers, partners, and entities accessing or using the Senseible platform ("User", "You").

## 1. Scope & Applicability

This Policy applies to all users, including visitors, registered users, and paid customers across India, emerging markets, and other jurisdictions where Senseible operates.

Senseible processes data as:

- (a) Data Fiduciary / Controller for direct users, and
- (b) Data Processor for enterprise, banking, or institutional customers where contractually agreed.

This Policy is designed to align with:

- India Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
- GDPR principles (where applicable)
- Emerging market data protection regimes

## 2. Data We Collect

Senseible may collect and process the following categories of data:

**(a) Business & Financial Data**

GST details, invoices, supply chain data, transactional records, operational metrics, accounting data.

**(b) Sustainability & ESG Data**

Activity data, emissions-related information, IoT/satellites data, derived sustainability indicators.

**(c) Voice & Interaction Data**

Voice inputs, transcripts, conversational logs used for onboarding and support.

**(d) Technical & Usage Data**

Device information, IP address, logs, access timestamps, platform usage metrics and patterns.

**(e) Account & Identity Data**

Name, email, authentication credentials, organization details.

**(f) Requests You Make**

When you ask for a report, guide or other resource: your name, work email, company, industry, role, the reason you give, and whether you agreed to receive updates.

**(g) Stories and Feedback You Share**

When you tell us about your experience: your answers, any quote you choose to add, your attribution choice (named, company only, or anonymous), and a record of your consent (what you agreed to, the version of the text shown, and when). If you are signed in and choose to, figures already in your account (such as verified emissions by scope and the number of documents processed) are added to your story.

## 3. Purpose of Data Processing

Data is processed strictly for:

- Carbon and emissions estimation
- ESG intelligence and reporting
- Climate finance, carbon monetization enablement and credit eligibility signals
- Fraud, anomaly, risk, and misuse detection
- Platform security and integrity
- Improving AI carbon models, services, and platform performance

Data is also processed for the following, each only as described:

- Sending you a resource you asked for, confirming that your email address can receive it, and understanding who uses our resources.
- Sending you updates about Senseible, only if you separately agree. You can withdraw at any time.
- Improving Senseible using the feedback you give.
- Publishing your story as a case study, only with your separate, explicit consent, in the form and attribution you approved.

## 4. AI Processing & Model Training

Senseible employs AI systems including OCR, large language models, anomaly detection, and proprietary emission inference models.

- (a) Unauthenticated user data is retained for up to three (3) months.
- (b) Authenticated or paid-user data is retained for up to eighteen (18) months.
- (c) Data may be used to train, fine-tune, and improve proprietary AI systems.
- (d) Users may opt out of future AI training by written notice. By emailing impact@senseible.earth.
- (e) Aggregated, anonymized, and learned model parameters are irreversible and non-deletable.

## 5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Senseible does not sell personal or business data.

Data may be disclosed only:

- to enable green loans, registries, partners, financing, climate finance, or carbon credit transactions,
- to comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

A story you consent to publish becomes public on senseible.earth and may be shared on Senseible's own channels. Once something is public, copies made by others (for example search engines or social shares) cannot be fully recalled.

## 6. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored using managed cloud infrastructure with access controls and safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access or misuse.

Senseible applies zero-trust principles to enterprise data paths. Access is explicitly authorized, scoped to the relevant account or organization, limited by role and purpose, and logged where the platform supports an audit record. Zero-trust describes an architectural approach and is not presented as a third-party certification.

Controls include:

- authenticated and role-scoped access,
- tenant-scoped database policies,
- private document storage,
- evidence fingerprints and audit references,
- monitoring and incident-response procedures.

## 7. Legal Basis & Compliance

Senseible processes data in compliance with:

- India Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
- Applicable emerging market data laws
- GDPR principles where applicable

Processing is based on user consent, contractual necessity, or legitimate interest.

## 8. Data Subject / User Rights

Subject to applicable law, Users may:

- request access or correction of data,
- request deletion of identifiable personal data,
- withdraw consent for AI training,
- raise grievances or complaints.

Certain rights may be limited where data has been anonymized or aggregated.

Users may also:

- withdraw consent to publication of their story at any time. We take it down from senseible.earth within seven (7) business days and stop using it in new material,
- change their attribution to company only or anonymous at any time.

## 9. Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for stated purposes, contractual obligations, or legal requirements. The default periods described in this Policy are up to three (3) months for unauthenticated data and up to eighteen (18) months for authenticated or paid-user data.

Upon termination, data may be deleted or anonymized except where retention is legally required or incorporated into aggregated intelligence. Retention periods may be extended for regulatory, audit, training, or dispute resolution purposes.

Details given to request a resource are kept for up to ninety (90) days, or up to eighteen (18) months when the request comes from a signed-in account, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Feedback that is not published is kept under the default periods above. A published story is kept while it is published and deleted within thirty (30) days of withdrawal. The record of your consent (what you agreed to and when, not the story itself) is kept for three (3) years after withdrawal, as proof that the story was published with your permission.

Senseible uses service providers only where needed to operate the platform. Current provider categories and confirmed services include:

The services involved depend on the features a User chooses to use. Providers process data under their applicable contractual and privacy terms. Senseible reviews provider access by purpose and does not authorize providers to sell User business data. Material changes relevant to enterprise processing will be reflected in this Policy or the applicable agreement.
