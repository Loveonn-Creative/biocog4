# Content Test

Owner rule, October 2026. Apply to all existing page copy and to every new page, UI label, email and Help article before it ships.

## Write like this
A sharp founder who understands the customer, using direct-response and SaaS writing principles: specific problems, clear value, natural language, zero friction, honest claims.

## Never
Generic hooks, polished corporate language, AI clichés, fabricated proof, forced emotion, unnecessary words, internal or technical terms a visitor would not use, vague or weak lines, explanations nobody asked for.

## The four questions
1. What should a visitor or buyer feel after reading this?
2. Does the copy trigger that exact emotion?
3. What is the page's primary goal?
4. Does every word support that goal?

## Before accepting any copy
Will our core audience instantly understand it, feel it is relevant to them, trust it, and know what to do next?

If any answer is no, rewrite it in natural, concise, user-facing language that creates the intended feeling and moves the reader toward the page's goal. Keep rewriting until it sounds human, credible and worth their attention.
