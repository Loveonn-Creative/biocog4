# Proposed policy amendments, October 2026 (for owner approval)

**Status: approved by the owner and applied on 16 October 2026** as additions only: no existing clause was changed. The text before the change is kept verbatim in `docs/legal/archive/legalContent.before-2026-10-16.ts.txt`.

Covers two new flows:
- **A. Requested resources:** the sample ESG report form at `/esg-report-sample`.
- **B. Customer stories:** the feedback journey that becomes a published case study once approved.

Why an amendment is needed: Privacy §3 says data is processed "strictly for" a list that covers neither flow, and nothing in either policy covers publishing customer content, names or logos.

---

## Privacy Policy

### §2 Data We Collect: add

> **(f) Requests You Make**
> When you ask for a report, guide or other resource: your name, work email, company, industry, role, the reason you give, and whether you agreed to receive updates.
>
> **(g) Stories and Feedback You Share**
> When you tell us about your experience: your answers, any quote or photo you choose to add, your attribution choice (named, company only, or anonymous), and a record of your consent (what you agreed to, the version of the text shown, and when). If you are signed in and choose to, figures already in your account (such as verified emissions by scope and the number of documents processed) are added to your story.

### §3 Purpose of Data Processing: add

> - Sending you a resource you asked for, confirming that your email address can receive it, and understanding who uses our resources.
> - Sending you updates about Senseible, only if you separately agree. You can withdraw at any time.
> - Improving Senseible using the feedback you give.
> - Publishing your story as a case study, only with your separate, explicit consent, in the form and attribution you approved.

### §5 Data Sharing & Disclosure: add

> - A story you consent to publish becomes public on senseible.earth and may be shared on Senseible's own channels. Once something is public, copies made by others (for example search engines or social shares) cannot be fully recalled.

### §8 Data Subject / User Rights: add

> - withdraw consent to publication of your story at any time. We take it down from senseible.earth within seven (7) business days and stop using it in new material,
> - change your attribution to company only or anonymous at any time.

### §9 Data Retention & Deletion: add

> Details given to request a resource are kept for up to ninety (90) days, or up to eighteen (18) months when the request comes from a signed-in account, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
>
> Feedback that is not published is kept under the default periods above. A published story is kept while it is published and deleted within thirty (30) days of withdrawal. The record of your consent (what you agreed to and when, not the story itself) is kept for three (3) years after withdrawal, as proof that the story was published with your permission.

## Terms of Service

### New §4A Customer Stories and Feedback

> **(a) Your choice.** Sharing feedback is optional and unpaid. Nothing is published without your explicit consent at the time you submit. If Senseible ever offers an incentive for a story, that will be disclosed on the story itself.
>
> **(b) What you approve.** Before you submit, you see the story exactly as it will appear. You choose to be named, to show only your company, or to stay anonymous. Senseible may shorten text or correct spelling. If any other change would alter what the story says, Senseible will ask you to approve it again.
>
> **(c) Licence.** By consenting, you grant Senseible a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to publish the approved story, and (if you chose) your name, company name and logo, on senseible.earth and Senseible's own channels. The licence lasts until you withdraw. Withdrawal ends future use; it cannot recall copies already made by others.
>
> **(d) Accuracy and authority.** You confirm that what you share is true to the best of your knowledge. If you choose to name your company, you confirm that you are authorised to approve this on its behalf.
>
> **(e) How results are shown.** Every figure in a story is labelled as one of:
> - reported by you;
> - calculated by Senseible from your documents;
> - verified (with who verified it);
> - estimated.
>
> A story describes one customer's experience. It does not guarantee similar results, a loan, credit approval, carbon credits or regulatory acceptance.
>
> **(f) Withdrawal.** Email the Data Officer (sneha@senseible.earth) or use the link in your confirmation email. The story comes down from senseible.earth within seven (7) business days.

## Notes for review
- **India:**
  - DPDP Act 2023 §5 and §6 ask for notice at collection, consent for each specific purpose, and withdrawal as easy as giving consent. The separate boxes and the withdrawal link in the confirmation email meet that.
  - ASCI's guidelines on testimonials ask for genuine, verifiable claims and disclosure of any material connection; (a) and (e) cover this.
- **EU visitors (GDPR):** explicit, granular, withdrawable consent, which this provides.
- **Retention:** the three-year period for consent records is a proposal. Shorten it if you prefer, but some proof period is needed in case a published story is ever disputed.
